The system uses a two-agent architecture. The Patient Bot is a live Pipecat pipeline that connects Telnyx or Twilio media streams to Deepgram STT, Claude patient reasoning, Cartesia TTS, and audio back to the phone call; it makes outbound calls and stays fully in character as one of the 10 patient scenarios. The Judge Bot is a separate post-call Claude evaluation step that reviews the transcript against the scenario expectations and bug conditions. Keeping them separate matters because the patient bot must never self-evaluate or reveal the test during the call, while the judge needs explicit quality-assurance instructions and evidence-backed analysis.

The key design choices optimize for natural calls and useful evidence. Pipecat handles the hard real-time pieces - audio serialization, turn-taking, interruptions, and transport plumbing - better than raw WebSocket code. Deepgram is used for live STT because speed matters during the call, while AssemblyAI is used post-call for a cleaner speaker-labeled transcript. The modular pipeline is used instead of a black-box speech-to-speech API because scenario logic, hidden trap timing, and conversation steering need explicit per-turn control. Cartesia is used for low-latency TTS because small response delays are often the difference between a natural phone call and an obvious bot.
